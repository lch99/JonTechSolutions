FAQ

Common questions

What is AI automation for SMEs?

It's using AI models like GPT or Claude to handle specific, repetitive tasks — answering common customer questions, extracting data from documents, or summarizing reports — so your team spends time on judgment calls instead of repetitive work.

Is AI automation expensive to set up?

Cost depends on scope, but most SME automations, like WhatsApp inquiry triage or document data extraction, are far cheaper than hiring additional staff to do the same repetitive task manually.

Will AI replace my staff?

No — the goal is to remove repetitive busywork so your existing team can focus on higher-value work, not to replace judgment-based roles.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

We build in human review and fallback steps for anything customer-facing or high-stakes, so mistakes get caught before they matter.
