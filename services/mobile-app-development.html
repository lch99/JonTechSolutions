FAQ

Common questions

How much does a mobile app cost in Malaysia?

Cost depends on platform coverage, offline requirements, and backend complexity. Most SME field-ops or customer-facing apps get a fixed quote after a free scoping call.

Do you build for both iOS and Android?

Yes — using React Native we build a single codebase that ships to both platforms, which keeps cost and maintenance lower than building two separate native apps.

Can the app work without internet access?

Yes, for field-use apps we design offline-first, syncing data automatically once connectivity returns.

Do you handle App Store and Play Store submission?

Yes, we handle the submission process for both stores and can manage ongoing updates after launch.
